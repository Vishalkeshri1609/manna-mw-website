Manna's Mens Wear – Efficient Apparel Sourcing & Procurement Solutions

At Manna's Mens Wear, sourcing and procurement are at the core of our operations. With over 26 years of experience in the apparel industry, we have developed a reliable and efficient system for acquiring high-quality garments that meet diverse business needs.

We specialize in sourcing ready-made uniform garments and t-shirts for retailers, wholesalers, and merchandisers. Our procurement strategy is built on strong supplier relationships, quality control, and streamlined logistics.

Our Approach to Sourcing

Our sourcing process ensures every product meets high standards of quality, durability, and comfort. We collaborate with trusted manufacturers to maintain consistency across all orders.

By evaluating materials, production methods, and finishing processes, we ensure reliable and competitively priced garments.

Procurement Process

  1. Requirement Analysis – Understanding product type, quantity, fabric preferences, and usage.
  2. Supplier Selection – Sourcing from trusted and verified manufacturers.
  3. Product Evaluation – Assessing quality, fit, durability, and consistency.
  4. Cost Optimization – Negotiating competitive pricing while maintaining quality.
  5. Order Management – Handling order placement, tracking, and coordination.
  6. Inventory Control – Maintaining efficient inventory systems for quick turnaround.
  7. Delivery & Logistics – Ensuring timely delivery and smooth logistics coordination.
