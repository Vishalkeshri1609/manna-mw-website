Manna Mens Wear – Reliable Apparel Export Solutions

At Manna Mens Wear, we follow a structured and transparent export process to ensure smooth international transactions, consistent product quality, and timely delivery. Our export workflow is designed to simplify complexities for our clients, allowing them to focus on growing their business while we handle sourcing, documentation, and logistics.

Phase 1: Planning & Confirmation

1. Requirement Understanding & Inquiry

  • Product type (t-shirts, uniforms, etc.)
  • Fabric, size, and fit specifications
  • Quantity and order frequency
  • Target market and usage

2. Product Selection & Sampling

  • Product recommendations based on needs
  • Sample development and approval
  • Quality and fit confirmation
  • Customization discussion

3. Pricing & Quotation

  • Transparent cost breakdown
  • Bulk pricing advantages
  • Flexible quotation based on volume
  • No hidden charges

4. Order Confirmation

  • Final product specifications
  • Quantity and delivery timelines
  • Payment terms agreement
  • Proforma invoice issuance

Phase 2: Execution & Fulfillment

5. Production & Quality Control

  • Material sourcing and production planning
  • Continuous quality checks
  • Consistency across bulk orders
  • Final inspection before dispatch

6. Packaging & Documentation

  • Secure and standardized packaging
  • Labeling as per international requirements
  • Invoice, packing list, and export documents
  • Compliance with export regulations

7. Shipping & Logistics

  • Coordination with logistics partners
  • Air or sea freight options
  • Shipment tracking and updates
  • On-time dispatch and delivery

8. Delivery & Post-Sales Support

  • Confirmation of shipment receipt
  • Support for any queries or issues
  • Assistance with repeat orders
  • Long-term partnership support

Key Benefits of Our Export Process

  • Smooth and transparent end-to-end workflow
  • Reliable quality control and consistency
  • Timely delivery with trusted logistics partners
  • Clear communication at every stage
  • Scalable solutions for bulk and repeat orders

FAQ – Export Process

What is the export process followed by Manna Mens Wear?
We follow a structured process including requirement analysis, sampling, pricing, order confirmation, production, quality checks, documentation, shipping, and post-sales support.
Do you provide samples before bulk export orders?
Yes, we provide samples for approval to ensure quality and specifications meet your requirements.
How do you ensure quality in export orders?
We implement strict quality control at every stage, including final inspection before shipment.
What types of garments do you export?
We export ready-made uniform garments and t-shirts for retail, corporate, and promotional use.
Do you handle export documentation?
Yes, we manage all export documentation including invoices and packing lists.
What shipping options are available?
We offer both air and sea freight options based on requirements.
How long does the export process take?
Timelines vary depending on order size and destination.
Can you handle large bulk export orders?
Yes, we specialize in managing large-scale orders efficiently.
Do you offer support after delivery?
Yes, we provide post-sales support and assistance for repeat orders.
Why choose Manna Mens Wear for garment export?
With 26+ years of experience and a reliable supply chain, we are a trusted export partner.
